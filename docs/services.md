# ARiiSE services

This document is the approved service-content source for the ARiiSE website. Final availability depends on the institution’s requirements and project scope.

## Moodle LMS implementation

- Moodle LMS setup for colleges, academies, and other educational institutions.
- Course, role, assessment, and learning-content configuration.
- Live-class integration planning for BigBlueButton, Google Meet, or Zoom.
- Teacher and administrator onboarding.

## AIMS workflow configuration

- Admission management, course allocation, internal marks, attendance, timetables, and reporting modules.
- Institution-specific academic and administrative workflow configuration.
- Student, teacher, administrator, and other role-based access arrangements.

## Branding, content, and custom development

- Institution-branded Moodle themes and user journeys.
- Digital-learning content preparation support for teachers when included in the scope.
- Custom Moodle modules, applications, and workflow extensions where standard features do not meet the requirement.

## Migration and integrations

- Migration planning for an existing LMS or academic platform.
- WhatsApp notification integration.
- eSSL biometric-device integration for attendance synchronisation.
- Other approved integrations that support a defined institutional workflow.

## Deployment and ongoing care

- ARiiSE-managed hosting or AWS, DigitalOcean, Hostinger, and local-vendor deployment based on the institution’s requirements.
- Hosting and backup planning.
- Maintenance, upgrade, and technical-support arrangements.

## Suitable institutions

- Colleges and higher-education institutions.
- Schools and academies that need a managed digital-learning platform.
- Online education providers.
- Corporate training programmes where Moodle and workflow configuration meet the training requirement.
